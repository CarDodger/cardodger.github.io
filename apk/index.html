CarDodger Companion for Android

HMS Core and Huawei Health are required. If either one is missing on your phone, the app will not work.

Why it is needed

The app uses Huawei’s Wear Engine to talk with your watch. That needs HMS Core and Huawei Health on your phone. Ads and in-app purchases also run on HMS Core.

Before you install

Install HMS Core from Huawei AppGallery, and make sure Huawei Health is installed with your watch paired. Then download the APK below and open it. Android may ask you to allow installs from this source.
