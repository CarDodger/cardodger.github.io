What stays on your device

The watch app stores your game progress (high scores, coins, purchased items, settings and license/trial status) in the watch’s local storage. We do not receive this data.

What we collect

CarDodger itself does not collect personal data and has no accounts or sign-up.

Watch ↔ phone sync

If you pair the apps, game status (such as unlocked items and rewards) is exchanged directly between your watch and phone over Bluetooth using Huawei Wear Engine. It does not pass through our servers.

Third-party services

  • Huawei HMS services (Petal Ads and In-App Purchases) in the companion app. They may process device identifiers and purchase information under Huawei’s own privacy policy.
  • App stores (Google Play, Huawei AppGallery) process purchases and downloads under their own policies.
