Dodge traffic from your wrist.

An arcade dodger built for the Huawei Watch Fit 4. Pair the phone app for rewards and unlocks.

Get the app How it plays

Built for a small screen.

Crown and swipe

Swipe or turn the crown to change lanes. Haptics are on by default and one switch turns them off.

Every point is a coin

Score one, bank one. Coins are saved on your watch and carry over between runs.

Crash and keep going

Losing a heart clears the road and gives you two seconds of invincibility.

Five skins

Each car brings its own coin multiplier and hearts.

Pair it with your phone.

The phone app syncs purchases and rewards to your watch over Bluetooth.
